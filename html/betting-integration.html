Plan: pick in Bet Hope, stake on a betting platform

Can a pick made here be staked directly on a real betting platform? Yes, in two ways: a link that opens the bookmaker's bet slip already filled in, or direct placement through a betting exchange's API. This page compares them, says what each needs, and recommends an order. Nothing here has been built yet.

Investigated 6 Oct 2026 · approved 6 Oct · Steps 1–3 done (7 Oct; Step 3 adapted for Nigeria); Step 4 not possible from Nigeria

Read this first: the model has no proven edge over the bookmakers. On 8,086 unseen matches, v2's probabilities never beat the odds by 5+ points. Betting every outcome where it saw any edge returned −13.9% (Phase 4 log, row 12). The model is good at saying how likely each result is (50.3%, level with the bookmakers), but that isn't the same as making money. Any integration should therefore start with tracking only (paper betting), and real stakes should stay small, capped and manual until the tracked return is positive over many hundreds of bets.

Nigeria (your country)

  • Regulation: since the Supreme Court's November 2024 ruling, betting is licensed by each state (e.g. Lagos LSLGA; a reciprocity licence covers 22 states). The NLRC only covers the FCT (Mondaq, SOFTSWISS). Use a bookmaker licensed for your state.
  • Exchanges: Betfair restricts Nigeria (country list), so direct placement (Step 4) can't work.
  • Bookmakers: licensed locally: e.g. 1xBet Nigeria and Betway Nigeria (LSLGA), Betano (NLRC) (NLRC list), plus Bet9ja, SportyBet and BetKing. None offers a public API for pre-filled bet slips.

Nigerian bookmaker partner programmes (researched 7 Oct 2026)

Why this route: getting around geo-blocking with a VPN was ruled out. KYC, payment and VPN checks catch it, and the exchanges' terms let them void bets and keep balances. The legitimate way to get closer to one-click betting from Nigeria is through the bookmakers' own partner programmes.

Nigerian odds: no official odds feed was found for Bet9ja, SportyBet or BetKing. An open-source scraper, NaijaBet_Api, reads 1X2 and double-chance odds from Bet9ja, BetKing and NairaBet. Conflict of interest: revenue share pays you a cut of what referred players lose. That sits awkwardly with an app that tells people which bets look good, especially since the model has no proven edge. If you join, say so clearly in the app, keep betting tools optional, and check your state's rules on gambling advertising (e.g. LSLGA in Lagos).

Recommended order

Step 1Analytics up to date (about 1 day)
What
The Analytics fixes above. No new services or costs.
Step 2Live odds and best price, plus paper betting (about 3–4 days)
What
  • Odds: a new provider apps/data_ingestion/providers/the_odds_api.py. A scheduled job (e.g. 06:00 and 12:00 UTC, plus 90 minutes before kickoffs) stores each bookmaker's 1X2 and over/under prices and links in a new BookmakerPrice table. This also fixes "upcoming fixtures have no odds yet", which currently blocks value bets and the odds model for most of the week.
  • Value bets: measured against the best available price, not the average.
  • Paper betting: a PaperBet table logs every pick automatically: the price at the time, a 1-unit stake, and the settled profit or loss after the result arrives.
  • Analytics: a "Return if you had bet" panel with return %, profit in units and the number of bets, for all picks, top picks and value bets.
Credits
One odds call costs about 1 credit per region per market. 20 leagues × 2 regions × 2 markets × ~4 refreshes a day ≈ 9,600 a month, which fits the $30 plan. The leagues The Odds API doesn't cover keep using the football-data.co.uk odds.
Step 3"Bet at …" buttons (about 1–2 days)
What
On predictions, top picks, value bets and match pages: the best price and a button that opens that bookmaker's bet slip (option A). Each button links to the bookmaker you choose in Settings, and there's an option to hide all betting buttons. Every betting surface shows a responsible-gambling notice and an 18+ note. Bet Hope never asks for bookmaker passwords.
Step 4 (optional)Place bets on your own exchange account (about 4–5 days)
Only if
Step 2 shows a positive tracked return, betting exchanges are legal and available where you live, and this is for your account only.
What
  • Placing a bet: an admin-only "Place bet" button that shows the stake, the price and the potential loss, then asks you to confirm.
  • Server side: the backend logs in to the exchange (certificate login; the keys and certificate stored in AWS Secrets Manager, not in plain Lambda variables, which addresses SEC-02) and calls placeOrders. The order and its status are saved in a PlacedBet table.
  • Hard limits: a maximum stake per bet, a daily loss limit and a weekly loss limit, plus an off switch. A bet that would exceed a limit is refused on the server, not just in the page.
  • Testing: development uses Betfair's free delayed key; the live key goes in last.

Rules that apply whichever option you choose

  • Law where you live: sports betting, betting exchanges and gambling affiliate links are regulated differently in each country. Check that the bookmaker or exchange is licensed and available in your country before Step 3 or 4.
  • No money held: Bet Hope must never hold anyone's money or store bookmaker passwords. Deep links avoid both; Step 4 uses only your own exchange's official API keys.
  • Making it public (other people staking through the app) is a different project: a vendor licence, possibly a gambling or affiliate licence, user accounts, age checks and responsible-gambling tools. Keep it personal unless you decide otherwise.

  2. Is this for you only, or for other users of the app too?
